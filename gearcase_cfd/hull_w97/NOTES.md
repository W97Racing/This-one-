# W-97 hull side profile (hand-traced)

- Source: one side-view photo of the W-97 hydro under way (the 2800 px upscaled copy).
- Method: 16 points read from a gridded crop, not automatic segmentation.
- Scale: nose tip to transom bottom horizontal run = 16 ft 6 in (5.029 m). Gives 5.08 mm/px.
- Origin: transom bottom, x forward, y up. Units mm.
- Accuracy: roughly +/-10 px (about +/-50 mm). Photo is not square-on, so lengths are approximate.
- Not included: the gearcase/leg. It is not clearly resolved in this photo (spray and small size).
- Cockpit/deck top is taken from the white side panel, not the rider or engine cover.
