# Gearcase side profile (from one photo, DMR lower unit)

- Source: side view photo (4000x3000), part lying on foam case. Silhouette segmented by
  low saturation / high brightness, then approximated to 118 points.
- Origin: lowest-left corner of the silhouette box. x toward the prop shaft, y up. Units mm.
- Scale used: the silhouette's full height (top of the top mount to the bottom of the skeg)
  = 14 in (355.6 mm). Scale 0.6017 mm/px.
- Result: the nose tip to the visible shaft end is about 427 mm (17 in), and the shaft tip
  is cut off by the frame, so the real nose-to-shaft-tip length is longer still.
- CONFLICT: the user gave nose-to-shaft-tip = 14 in. The photo says that length is at least
  17 in at the height scale. Either the height reference is not the 14 in dimension, or the
  photo is foreshortened. If the 14 in length is right, the part is about 11.7 in tall or
  less at the same photo. Not resolved.
- Accuracy: about +/-5-10 mm (foam caught along the lower-left edge in a few places).
- Not included: the internal shaft and bearing housing; the overlay with the photo.
